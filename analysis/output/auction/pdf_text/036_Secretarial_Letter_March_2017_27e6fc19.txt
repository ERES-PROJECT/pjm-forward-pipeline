Dear Mr. Peoples:

           On March 23, 2017, CRA International, Inc. dba Charles River Associates filed
Duquesne Light Co.’s (DLC) results of its POLR VIII procurement process and rules, including the
proposed bid results for provider of last resort for all Residential and Small Commercial & Industrial
customers electing to receive default service from DLC during the twelve month period from June 1,
2017, to May 31, 2018, and for the twenty-four months period from June 1, 2017, to May 31, 2019;
Medium Commercial & Industrial customers electing to receive default service from DLC during the
three month period from June 1, 2017, to August 31, 2017, and Large Commercial & Industrial customers
electing to receive default service from DLC during the twelve month period from June 1, 2017, to
May 31, 2018.

          DLC requested that the Commission approve the bid results of the March 23, 2017 POLR VIII
process on or before March 27, 2017.

                Further investigation does not appear to be warranted at this time, since the results of the
March 2017 Solicitation appear to have been conducted in accordance with the RFP Process and Rules as
approved by the Commission.

                Therefore, we direct DLC to disclose the results of this March 23, 2017 Solicitation in accordance
with the Commission’s Secretarial Letter in Re: Disclosure of Default Service Solicitation Results and Creation of
a Default Service Rate Calculation Model, issued October 12, 2010 at Docket No. M-2009-2082042.
