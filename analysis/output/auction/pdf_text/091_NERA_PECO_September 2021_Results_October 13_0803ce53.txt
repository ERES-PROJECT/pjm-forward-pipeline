          Results of September 2021 Solicitation
                Under PECO’s DSP V

On September 30, 2021, the Pennsylvania Public Utility Commission approved the results of
the September 2021 Solicitation under PECO’s fifth Default Service Program (“DSP V”). The
Secretarial Letter is available on the PUC website.

The results of the September 2021 Solicitation are provided below in accordance with the
PUC’s directive dated October 12, 2010 (Docket No. M-2009-2082042).

PECO has posted the actual Price to Compare for the period September 1, 2021 – November 30,
2021 for all classes to its website. PECO has posted an estimated Price to Compare for the
period December 1, 2021 – February 28, 2022 for all classes.
