          Results of September 2019 Solicitation
               Under PECO’s DSP IV

On September 26, 2019, the Pennsylvania Public Utility Commission approved the results of
the September 2019 Solicitation under PECO’s fourth Default Service Program (“DSP IV”). The
Secretarial Letter is available on the PUC website.

The results of the September 2019 Solicitation are provided below in accordance with the
PUC’s directive dated October 12, 2010 (Docket No. M-2009-2082042).

PECO has posted the actual Price to Compare for the period September 1, 2019 – November 30,
2019 for all classes to its website. PECO has posted an estimated Price to Compare for the
period December 1, 2019 – February 29, 2020 for all classes.
