              Results of March 2025 Solicitation
                Under PECO’s DSP VI

2025 Solicitation under PECO’s sixth Default Service Program (“DSP VI”). The Secretarial Letter
is available on the PUC website.

The results of the March 2025 Solicitation are provided below in accordance with the PUC’s
directive dated October 12, 2010 (Docket No. M-2009-2082042).
