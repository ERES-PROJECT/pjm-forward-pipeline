            Results of March 2018 Solicitation
               Under PECO’s DSP IV

On March 15, 2018, the Pennsylvania Public Utility Commission approved the results of the
March 2018 Solicitation under PECO’s fourth Default Service Program (“DSP IV”). The
Secretarial Letter is available on the PUC website.

The results of the March 2018 Solicitation are provided below in accordance with the PUC’s
directive dated October 12, 2010 (Docket No. M-2009-2082042).

PECO has posted the actual Price to Compare for the period March 1, 2018 – May 31, 2018 for
all classes to its website. PECO has posted an estimated Price to Compare for the period June 1,
2018 – August 31, 2018 for all classes.
