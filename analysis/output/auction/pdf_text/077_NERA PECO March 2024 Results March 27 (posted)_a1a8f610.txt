            Results of March 2024 Solicitation
                Under PECO’s DSP V

On March 14, 2024, the Pennsylvania Public Utility Commission approved the results of the
March 2024 Solicitation under PECO’s fifth Default Service Program (“DSP V”). The Secretarial
Letter is available on the PUC website.

The results of the March 2024 Solicitation are provided below in accordance with the PUC’s
directive dated October 12, 2010 (Docket No. M-2009-2082042).
