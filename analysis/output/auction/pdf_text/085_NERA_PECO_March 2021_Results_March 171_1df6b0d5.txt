            Results of March 2021 Solicitation
                Under PECO’s DSP V

On March 4, 2021, the Pennsylvania Public Utility Commission approved the results of the
March 2021 Solicitation under PECO’s fifth Default Service Program (“DSP V”). The Secretarial
Letter is available on the PUC website.

The results of the March 2021 Solicitation are provided below in accordance with the PUC’s
directive dated October 12, 2010 (Docket No. M-2009-2082042).

PECO has posted the actual Price to Compare for the period March 1, 2021 – May 31, 2021 for
all classes to its website. PECO has posted an estimated Price to Compare for the period June 1,
2021 – August 31, 2021 for all classes.
